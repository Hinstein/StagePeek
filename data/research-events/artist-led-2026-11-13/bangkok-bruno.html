    Global superstar Bruno Mars has expanded his record-breaking The Romantic Tour once again, announcing 26 new stadium shows across Australia, New Zealand, and Asia in 2027. Mars will return to Thailand with two nights at Rajamangala National Stadium in April.

    9x GRAMMY® Award winner and Silk Sonic collaborator Anderson .Paak will perform as DJ Pee .Wee on all newly announced shows.

    The tour follows the arrival of Bruno Mars’ long-awaited fourth solo album, The Romantic, which was the biggest debut album of his career.

    FAQ

    How much do tickets cost for Bruno Mars - The Romantic Tour ?

    Ticket Prices for this event are as follows:

    Seated: 2,800-9,300 THB

    Standing: 3,900 THB

    Ticket Prices exclude Booking Fee, which are 3%. Prices vary by location and availability, and quantities are limited. All tickets and ticket types are subject to availability and may sell out.

     

    When do tickets go on sale for Bruno Mars - The Romantic Tour ? 

    • Artist & Live Nation Presale: 10:00 AM on Sat 12th Sep to 10:00 AM on Sun 13th Sep
    • General Sale: 12:00 PM on Mon 14th Sep

    *All times are local  

    Who is eligible for the Bruno Mars - The Romantic Tour Presale? 

    Artist Presale: Sign up for early access at BrunoMars.com. Registration closes on Monday, September 7th. You'll receive an email by the morning of Friday, September 11th with the presale details.

    Live Nation Presale: This presale is for Live Nation members only. On Live Nation presale date, login to livenationtero.co.th and select the concert you want to purchase then click ‘Buy Tickets’. (‘Buy Tickets’ button will only show on the presale date)

    ** Signing up for Artist Presale or Live Nation Tero membership to access the presale does not guarantee that you will be able to purchase tickets. All tickets and ticket types are subject to availability and may sell out.

    Do I need a code to access the General Sale for Bruno Mars - The Romantic Tour ?

    No. To access the General Sale, you just need to be signed into your Ticketmaster.co.th account.  

    What is the ticket limit for Bruno Mars - The Romantic Tour ?

    You may purchase a maximum of 6 tickets per transaction during the Presale and General Sale.

    Please adhere to the published ticket limit. Persons who exceed the ticket limit may have any or all of their orders and tickets cancelled without notice by Ticketmaster at its discretion. This includes orders associated with the same name, e-mail address, billing address, credit card number or any other information.

    How will I get my ticket for Bruno Mars - The Romantic Tour  ?

    Tickets for this tour will be mobile-only. You can access your tickets via mobile web browser at Ticketmaster.co.th.

    How can I get ready for the Bruno Mars - The Romantic Tour ticket sale?

    You can join the waiting room up to 60 minutes before the sale begins. We recommend using one browser on one device. You can have separate tabs open for different shows, but avoid opening multiple tabs for the same show.

    When the sale starts, you’ll be placed in the queue and will be able to see your position. Do not refresh the page while you’re in the queue or during the purchase process, as this may cause you to lose your place or be flagged as a bot.

    When selecting tickets, you will have the choice between Best Available Seating or Choose Your Own Seats. For shows in high demand, the quickest way to ensure you secure tickets in your preferred category or section is to choose Best Available Seating. 

    Ticket availability can change as other fans complete their purchases. The seat map will update automatically, so there’s no need to refresh the page.

    If you need further assistance, contact us via the help page on our website.

    How do I select my seats using Best Available?

    Best Available is the quickest way to secure seats. Rather than choosing a specific row and seat number, you choose where you want to sit and how many tickets you need, and our system allocates the best seats currently available to you.

    1. Select the section you would like to purchase tickets from.
    2. Choose the quantity of tickets you need.
    3. Select the Best Available option.
    4. Our system will allocate you the next best seats available in that section.
    5. Confirm your selection and proceed with the purchase process.

    What are the conditions under the Ticket Purchase & Entry Policy?

    Each attendee can have their name on only one ticket per show. The full name (in English only) must match the valid ID presented at the event — such as a national ID, driver’s license, or passport. (1 name per 1 ticket per 1 show). If multiple tickets are purchased, each ticket must list a different attendee.

    Names on tickets cannot be changed, edited, or transferred under any circumstances. If duplicate names are found for the same show, or if the name does not match the ID, the organizer reserves the right to deny entry without refund.

    For VIP tickets or tickets with special privileges, if the name on the ticket does not match the ID, the organizer reserves the right to deny all access and entitlements with no refund under any circumstances.

    Any transactions found to involve bots, multiple account creation, or actions that exceed the ticket limit or suggest suspicious behavior will be cancelled without prior notice.

    Important: The name on the ticket must exactly match ID/passport. No transfers or changes allowed.

    What are the risks of buying or reselling tickets through unofficial sources?

    Buying or reselling tickets through unofficial sources — whether online or offline — may lead to your tickets being immediately canceled and invalid for entry. Tickets purchased from third parties or unauthorized sellers (such as Viagogo or online auction sites) may be considered void. If this happens, you may be denied entry to the event, lose access to any included benefits or perks, and all related orders under the same account may be canceled without any refund.

    What are the age restrictions?

    Children under 12 are not allowed in the standing zone and children under 7 years of age are not recommended to attend the show. Those under 16 should be accompanied by a guardian.

The same rule applies: each attendee can have only one ticket per show under their name.

    If duplicate names are found, or the name on the ticket does not match the identification presented, the organizer reserves the right to deny entry without a refund and holds no responsibility for any issues that may arise.

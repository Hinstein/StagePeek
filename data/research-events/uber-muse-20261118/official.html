Muse live at Uber Arena

Within their "The Wow! Signal Europe Tour " Muse will perform one of their spectacular live shows at the Uber Arena in Berlin on November 18, 2026.

Tickets are available now.

The British rock trio Muse is difficult to categorize into a specific genre. In their songs, the band combines a wide variety of elements from alternative rock, progressive rock, and electronic music. In addition to their unique sound, Muse captivates audiences above all with their extraordinary live performances. Their concerts in large venues like the Uber Arena are characterized by massive light shows, extravagant stage designs, and a phenomenal sound experience.

DIAMOND BALL ROOM

Highlight of the premium experience at the Uber Arena is the Amazon Music DIAMOND BALL ROOM. Expect a perfect view of the stage coupled with a noble bar atmosphere. Furnished in the style of a modern Private Member Club, the Amazon Music DIAMOND BALL ROOM is equipped with 72 seats which can be booked individually. The furnishings are handmade and - together with the subtle ambient lighting - make for a truly special atmosphere. The cocktails and long drinks are mixed freshly by the bartender, the gourmet catering – which is inspired by seasons -  is served throughout your entire visit at the Amazon Music DIAMOND BALL ROOM, even during the show. Thanks to a Bose sound system, the after show party can start right after the event.

More comfort, more possibilities and more of the very best the Uber Arena has to offer - the Amazon Music DIAMOND BALL ROOM.

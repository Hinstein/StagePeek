2026 KUN「The Deadman」- Hong Kong

By Idea Entertainment Asia (HK) Limited
28 - 29 Nov 2026
AsiaWorld-Arena (Hall 1) View Seating Map

Event Details

Following its stops in Macao and Singapore, the 2026 KUN “The Deadman” Tour continues its journey. Now, this musical exploration of self, fate, and rebirth arrives in Hong Kong, bringing its story to a new city and a wider audience.

Over the past year, KUN has continued to look inward while building connections with the world through music. From the releases of “Deadman,” “Jasmine,” “What a Day,” and “Colder,” to the final unveiling of his self-titled album KUN, he has moved from capturing individual moments of emotion through each song to shaping a complete narrative across an entire album. Step by step, he has developed a musical expression distinctly his own, inviting listeners closer to a more authentic and deeper sense of self.

Throughout his creative journey, KUN has remained committed to following his own path. Rather than responding to outside expectations, he chooses to search inward for answers, capturing emotions that may be subtle and fleeting, yet deeply real and intense. His music brings together the raw energy of roots-driven rhythms, the warmth and texture of the vinyl era, and the precision and edge of contemporary digital sound. By stripping away unnecessary embellishment, he allows the music to return to its purest form of resonance. And because of this, these songs are never simply expressions of a single moment—they take on new life with every encounter.

KUN has carried these songs across eight cities in Europe and North America. What began as deeply personal expressions found their way into vast and unfamiliar places, where they were heard, felt, and given new echoes. On stage, melodies are no longer simply performed; they become an intimate yet genuine connection between people. Emotions of love, loss, longing, resilience, and loneliness are given new life through each shared moment of resonance. And as this inward exploration continues, these emotions gradually come together to form a larger, more complete story.

Deadman is not simply an expression—it is an entry point, leading slowly into the deeper reaches of the self. At its heart is a quiet meditation on finding life through the confrontation with death: a person repeatedly torn apart by obsession, desire, and fate, yet each time attempting to awaken once again. From this struggle, a world suspended between reality and illusion gradually emerges, while emotions that cannot be fully captured in words continue to unfold through sound and light.

Now, “The Deadman” Tour takes the story into a new chapter, transforming this inner world into a Western epic shaped by an inescapable sense of fate.

Step into the Western wasteland, where wind and sand close in to form an arena that seems to exist for him alone. Here, the confrontation with fate no longer takes place somewhere beyond us. Instead, it unfolds within the inner wasteland that has always existed in each of us, yet is rarely confronted.

As the dust rises once more and the wheels of fate begin to turn again, a break from the former self begins...

On November 28th-29th , 2026, KUN “The Deadman” — Hong Kong invites everyone willing to feel and listen to step into this raw yet deeply human world—and, amid the raging wind and storm, move closer to a truer version of themselves.

Admission Arrangement

Arrangement for Seating Zone 
  • Audiences are encouraged not to bring bags/backpacks to the event hall. Express Lanes for admission are available for audiences NOT carrying bags/backpacks (if applicable).
  • All audiences are required to go through handheld metal detector checking before entering the event hall (if applicable).
  • For audience who would like to re-enter the venue, please present the re-entry token together with the original concert admission ticket to our admission staff. AWE reserves the right to amend the admission procedures from time to time.
 
The use of wheelchairs or electric wheelchairs on AWE premises is subject to the following conditions:
 
  • Wheelchair seat tickets are designated for persons who depend on wheelchair for mobility and their accompanying minders (if applicable). When purchasing wheelchair seat tickets, each wheelchair user is entitled to purchase a maximum of one minder at the same time. Wheelchair seat ticket holders must produce proof of mobility difficulties* upon demand by AWEM during admission.  AWEM will refuse admission without refund, in case of non-wheelchair user or any person accompanying any non-wheelchair user holding wheelchair seat ticket or minder ticket for admission. AWEM and the event organiser reserve the right to have the final decision in case of any disputes.
* Proof of mobility difficulties means “Registration Card for People with Disabilities” (Physical Disability) or other valid medical documentary proof showing physical disability or mobility difficulties.
  • AsiaWorld-Expo Management Limited and the event organiser reserve the right to refuse entry if the ticket purchased is not fitted for the ticket holder’s specific seating requirements or accessibility needs, and there shall be no refund whatsoever.
  • Wheelchair users with tickets may contact AWE (+852-3606 8000) for admission assistance. They are also advised to arrive at the performance venue with sufficient lead time for admission.

2024 RIIZE FAN-CON ‘RIIZING DAY’ in HONG KONG

By Rolemodel Entertainment Group Limited
01 Jun 2024
Hall 8 & 10 View Seating Map

Event Details

Rolemodel Entertainment Group Limited (REG) is thrilled to announce SM Entertainment’s newest Korean boy group RIIZE taking the stage first time in Hong Kong on Saturday, 1st June 2024. 2024 RIIZE FAN-CON ‘RIIZING DAY’ in HONG KONG will be held at AsiaWorld-Expo, Hall 8 & 10.

RIIZE has been receiving considerable attention even before their debut, praised as a "monster rookie group" for their outstanding talent and charming appearance. They made their debut last September with the single album Get a Guitar, which sold over one million copies in its first week. Since then, with songs like “Get A Guitar”, “Memories”, “Talk Saxy”, “Love 119”, and “Siren”, they have consistently topped various music charts, solidifying their status as "super rookies".

The group name RIIZE is a combination of the English words “Rise” and “Realize”, symbolizing a team that grows together and achieves dreams. They recently announced the release of their first mini-album RIIZING in June and will gradually unveil the tracks from early April. Additionally, they have revealed plans to hold their first fan-con tour ‘RIIZING DAY’ starting in Seoul in May, followed by stops in Hong Kong, Tokyo, Taipei, and more. Fans can expect not only RIIZE's music and performances but also various sessions including games and talks during this tour. BRIIZE are urged not to let slip this golden opportunity to forge unforgettable memories alongside RIIZE.

Admission Arrangement

Arrangement for Seating Zone 
  • All audiences are required to use the main entrance of Hall 10 for admission.
  • All audiences are required to present the physical concert admission ticket for admission.
  • Audiences are encouraged not to bring bags/backpacks to the event hall.
  • All audiences are required to go through bag searches before entering the event hall.
  • For audience who would like to re-enter the venue, please present the re-entry token together with the original concert admission ticket to our admission staff. AWE reserves the right to amend the admission procedures from time to time.
 
 The use of wheelchairs or electric wheelchairs on AWE premises is subject to the following conditions:
  • Wheelchair seat tickets are designated for persons who depend on wheelchair for mobility and their accompanying minders. When purchasing wheelchair seat tickets, each wheelchair user is entitled to purchase a maximum of one minder at the same time. Wheelchair seat ticket holders must produce proof of mobility difficulties* upon demand by AWEM during admission.  AWEM will refuse admission without refund, in case of non-wheelchair user or any person accompanying any non-wheelchair user holding wheelchair seat ticket or minder ticket for admission. AWEM and the event organiser reserve the right to have the final decision in case of any disputes.
* Proof of mobility difficulties means “Registration Card for People with Disabilities” (Physical Disability) or other valid medical documentary proof showing physical disability or mobility difficulties.
  • Wheelchair users with tickets may contact AWE (+852-3606 8888) for admission assistance. They are also advised to arrive at the performance venue with sufficient lead time for admission.

Long umbrellas are not allowed in the event hall. Please place restricted article(s) at Baggage Storage Counter or self-service lockers at the Ground Floor Level.

Where can I park at SoFi Stadium?

Please visit our parking and transportation page for more information and purchase parking ahead of time for your event. Please plan to arrive early to your event at SoFi Stadium.

What items are prohibited at SoFi Stadium?

Please visit our stadium policies page for more information on SoFi Stadium's bag policy and prohibited items list.

Can I get a tour of SoFi Stadium?

SoFi Stadium tours are available to the public. Please visit our Tours page for more details and to purchase tickets. 

Can I bring in food or drink into SoFi Stadium?

Outside beverages are not permitted inside SoFi Stadium except one (1) factory-sealed 20 oz. or less water bottle, soft-sided single juice or milk containers, or medically required liquids in a sealed container. Please visit our Dining Guide page for more information on food and beverage options inside the stadium.

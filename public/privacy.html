隐私政策 / Privacy

版本 / Version：2026-10-26

处理哪些信息 / Information

账号功能处理邮箱、共享身份 ID、验证状态、本站条款接受记录、收藏位置，以及启用订阅时的套餐状态和支付平台标识。Google 登录仅请求必要身份信息。密码由共享 Supabase Auth 校验和存储，StagePeek 不在业务表保存明文密码。

Account features process email, shared identity ID, verification status, terms acceptance, saved positions and, when subscriptions are used, plan status and payment-provider references. Passwords are handled by shared Supabase Auth, not stored as plaintext in StagePeek business tables.

用途与服务 / Purpose and services

这些信息用于登录、安全验证、权限判断、保存位置和订阅管理。事务邮件使用 Brevo；认证由共享 Supabase Auth 提供；Google 与支付平台仅在你使用对应功能时参与。业务数据按项目与用户权限隔离。

Information supports sign-in, security, access, saved positions and billing. Transactional email uses Brevo and identity uses shared Supabase Auth. Google and payment providers participate when you use those features. Business data is scoped by project and user.

Cookie 与日志 / Cookies and logs

认证使用必要的 HttpOnly 安全 Cookie；短期验证流程绑定发起浏览器。我们不把密码、验证码或认证凭据写入分析事件或普通日志。限流记录用于防止滥用，过期验证数据定期清理。仅在本站已配置时加载流量统计；统计只记录规范化页面和匿名流程状态，不含邮箱、票面位置、查询参数、订单号、金额、支付资料或来源网址。

Necessary HttpOnly cookies carry authentication state. Temporary verification is browser-bound. Passwords, codes and credentials are excluded from analytics and ordinary logs. Expired verification and rate-limit records are cleaned periodically. When configured, analytics records normalized pages and anonymous funnel states only; it does not receive email, ticket location, query strings, order IDs, amounts, payment details or referrers.

你的选择 / Your choices

可不注册而浏览免费内容；可退出账号并管理已存位置和订阅。账号删除或访问请求通过 项目反馈入口提出，需先核实身份；不要公开提交个人敏感资料。共享身份删除可能影响其他平台产品，应先确认范围。本站尚无自助全平台账号删除功能。

You may browse free content without an account, sign out, and manage saved positions and subscriptions. Access or deletion requests require identity verification; do not post sensitive details publicly. Deleting a shared identity may affect other products. Platform-wide self-service deletion is not currently available.
